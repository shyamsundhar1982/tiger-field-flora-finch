# Phase 5 · Vayu Shastr Management OS

## Scope
- Management OS over the Phase 2 financial architecture, Phase 3 operational execution and Phase 4 intelligence layer.
- Venture-aware management context for Consolidated, Vyndi · Carbon Venture and Aluminium Bicycle Venture.
- Base, upside and downside planning envelopes.
- Governance readiness, controlled decisions and investor/board management snapshot.

## Control boundary
Phase 5 is management control and decision support. It does not replace statutory accounting, tax records, immutable inventory movements or authenticated approval/audit persistence.

## Acceptance gates
1. Venture boundaries remain explicit.
2. Scenario selection changes the planning envelope without mutating the accounting model.
3. Governance readiness is visible.
4. Management decisions require an explicit role and approval/rejection action.
5. Investor/board information is presented as a management view, not a statutory statement.
6. Phase 2–4 routes remain available and unchanged.

## Backend continuation
The next production hardening layer should persist approvals and audit events server-side, add permission-backed approval hierarchy, immutable event history, investor reporting snapshots and authenticated workflow execution.
