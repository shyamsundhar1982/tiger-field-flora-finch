# Phase 4 — Scale, Automation & Intelligence

## Objective
Move from Phase 3 transaction visibility to a management control loop that detects operational exceptions, quantifies their commercial consequence, proposes controlled actions, and keeps approval with management.

## Included in this release
- Venture-scoped inventory and demand signals.
- Automated shortage and reorder-point detection.
- Working-capital watch based on demand value.
- Action recommendations with an INR impact envelope.
- Explicit approve/reject management queue.
- Dedicated Management Intelligence command route.
- Carbon / Aluminium operating boundaries preserved.

## Control principle
Phase 4 is decision support, not autonomous purchasing, payment, or accounting. Recommendations must be approved before operational execution. Phase 3 remains the operational transaction layer; statutory accounting remains authoritative downstream.

## Next scale layer
A future backend can persist signals, approvals, supplier lead times, actual inventory, BOM consumption, cash availability, margin impact and audit history, then execute approved actions through authenticated transactional services.
