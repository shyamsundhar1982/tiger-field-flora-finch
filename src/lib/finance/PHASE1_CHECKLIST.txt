Phase 1 finance completion criteria
- Single source of truth for operating and accounting assumptions
- 36-month Plan / Forecast / Actual management view
- Timed AR/AP cash conversion
- Inventory, purchases, COGS and cash linkage
- P&L, Balance Sheet and Operating / Investing / Financing cash flow
- Funding classification support: equity / debt / grant
- GST and tax planning controls with CA-reconciliation warnings
- Automated balance-sheet and cash-flow integrity checks
- Scenario planning and cash-floor / runway controls
- Admin edit / viewer read-only command controls

Phase 2 intentionally remains outside this release: statutory ledger, invoice-level GST/tax, bank reconciliation, payroll/TDS, full loan amortisation and multi-user cloud actuals.
